Weather keyframes, modded capes in replays, camera presets that follow a moving target, and number inputs and sliders in the editor.

FlashbackNH is an unofficial, independent reimplementation of the feature set of Flashback. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Flashback or its author.

## New

- **Weather keyframes.** A new Weather track sets rain and thunder strength (each 0 to 1) over time, with the same interpolation types as the other tracks. While keys are on it overrides the weather setting in the Visuals panel.
- **Modded capes.** Capes from mods such as GregTech, Galacticraft and Galaxy Space now show in replays. Before, only vanilla capes did, because the mod packets or downloads that hand out these capes are not part of a replay. The recorder now saves the cape each player wears (`capes.json` in the replay), and capes that only exist as a download are saved as an image. This only works for replays recorded with 0.2.0 or later.
- **Camera presets** (Window > Camera Presets). A preset moves the camera with a player or mob while the replay plays, without keyframes:
  - *Orbit* circles the target at a set radius, height and speed.
  - *Follow* moves with the target at a set offset, which can turn with the target's heading.
  - *Look At* stays in one place and turns to keep the target in view.

  A preset covers a range of ticks and shows as a bar on the camera track. It starts from where the camera is when you make it, can smooth out the target's movement, and blends into the camera keyframes at both ends. Presets show in exports, and you can preview them while paused.
- **Number inputs and sliders.** You can drag any number in the editor sideways, or click it and type a value. Values with a natural range use sliders: FOV, time of day, camera roll and pitch, weather, shake, and preset radius and angles. Double-click or right-click a slider to type a value. Playback speed (next to the play button) and flying speed (Movement panel) can be typed in too.

## Install

Put `flashbacknh-0.2.0.jar` in your `mods` folder. It is client only. Do not use the `-dev` or `-sources` jars. Replays from 0.1.x still open; they just have no recorded mod capes.

## Known limits

- A preset needs its target in the world. While the target is out of range or dead, the camera stays where the target was last seen.
- Presets do not avoid blocks: an orbit or follow camera can pass through terrain. Adjust the radius, height or offset if it does.
- If a player changes capes during a recording, the replay shows the last cape for the whole replay.
- Mods that draw capes through their own render code, instead of changing the player's cape texture, are not covered.
- Everything listed for 0.1.1 still applies.
