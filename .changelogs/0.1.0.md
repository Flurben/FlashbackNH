First release of FlashbackNH, a client-side replay mod for GT New Horizons (Minecraft 1.7.10).

FlashbackNH is an unofficial, independent reimplementation of the feature set of Flashback. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Flashback or its author.

## Features

- Record in singleplayer and multiplayer, with pause, resume and markers.
- Watch replays with a free camera, timeline seeking and playback speed control.
- Edit camera paths and visuals with keyframes.
- Export video through ffmpeg, or PNG sequences without it.
- See Distant Horizons LODs in replays and exports, in singleplayer.

## Install

Put `flashbacknh-0.1.0.jar` in your `mods` folder. It is client only. Do not use the `-dev` or `-sources` jars.

## Known limits

This is an early release. It has been tested mostly in short recordings in a singleplayer test world, so please report what you find during play.

- Not tested much or at all: long recordings, large bases and public servers.
- Distant Horizons is tested only in the singleplayer overworld, with DH alpha18 and Angelica 2.1.16. The Angelica version in the stock pack is too old for DH.
- Starting a recording mid-session still causes a short freeze: about 0.3 s at 12 chunks render distance and about 0.9 s at 32.

See the README for the full list of tested versions and limitations.
