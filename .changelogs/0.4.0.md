Worn items from other mods in replays, smooth camera acceleration with adjustable easing, and undo/redo in the editor.

FlashbackNH is an unofficial, independent reimplementation of the feature set of Flashback. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Flashback or its author.

## New

- **Worn items from other mods.** What players wear or hold through other mods is now recorded with each player and shown on them in the replay, as it looked in the game:
  - Baubles, including Botania's cosmetic items, Extra Utilities' angel ring wings and other baubles drawn on the player
  - the Backhand offhand item
  - Galacticraft's oxygen gear, tanks and parachute
  - worn Adventure Backpacks and Backpacks

  Servers do not always send these to the player they belong to, and a recording that starts mid-session missed what they sent earlier, so FlashbackNH now stores them itself. They are kept for every player when a recording starts, and for you whenever they change.
- **Smooth camera acceleration.** Smooth camera movement no longer changes speed suddenly. A path starts and ends at rest instead of jumping to full speed. Where a Smooth segment meets a Linear or eased one, it takes that segment's speed.
- **Ease in and ease out.** Each keyframe has an **Ease in** and an **Ease out** slider in the Keyframe Inspector. They set how long the camera takes to slow into the keyframe and speed away from it. The speed at the keyframe stays the same, so a longer ease spreads the change of speed out. The default of 33% gives the same curve between keyframes as before. **Reset easing** puts it back. With several keyframes selected, the sliders change all of them.
- **Speed graph.** The inspector shows how fast the camera moves from the keyframe before the selected one to the one after, so sudden starts and jumps are easy to see.
- **Undo and redo.** Ctrl+Z undoes and Ctrl+Y (or Ctrl+Shift+Z) redoes, also from the new **Edit** menu. This covers keyframes, easing, in/out points, markers, visuals and camera presets. Dragging a keyframe or a slider counts as one step. Export settings are not part of it.

## Changes

- Camera paths made in earlier versions move the same way between keyframes, but now start and stop from rest at their first and last keyframe.
- Replays recorded with 0.4.0 cannot show worn items in earlier versions, which skip them. Older replays open as before, without worn items from the mods above.

## Install

Put `flashbacknh-0.4.0.jar` in your `mods` folder. It is client only. Do not use the `-dev` or `-sources` jars.

## Known limits

- Galacticraft draws the oxygen mask only on your own player. It shows in third person in the game, but not in the replay, which shows you the way other players see you on a server. The tanks and other gear do show.
- Cosmetic armor mods that are not in GTNH, such as Cosmetic Armor, are not supported.
- Tested in singleplayer. Worn items on a server and on other players were not tested.
- Not tested in the game: Tinkers' Construct, Thaumcraft, Witching Gadgets, Draconic Evolution, EnderIO Dark Steel upgrades, OpenBlocks, Hardcore Ender Expansion, Gadomancy and Iguana Tweaks. These also draw on players, mostly from armor or held items, which replays already show.
- The undo, redo and easing actions were tested in the editor, but not with a real mouse and keyboard, so the Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y keys themselves were not pressed.
- Everything listed for 0.3.1 still applies.

See the README for the full list of tested versions and limitations.
